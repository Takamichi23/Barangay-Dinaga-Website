How to Apply for a Barangay Document

Barangay documents certify your identity, residency, or financial status. These are commonly required for job applications, government permits, scholarships, and legal transactions.

  1. Valid government-issued ID (e.g., PhilSys National ID, Passport, Driver’s License, Voter’s ID).
  2. Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill, lease agreement, or barangay residency record).
  3. Recent 1×1 or 2×2 ID photo (when required for specific clearances).
  4. Applicable processing fee (varies depending on the document type; indigency certificates are typically free).
  1. Proceed to the Barangay Dinaga Hall during regular office hours.
  2. Inform the desk staff about the specific document or clearance you need.
  1. Fill out the request form provided by the Barangay Secretary’s desk accurately.
  2. Clearly state your purpose for requesting the document.
  1. Hand in your completed form together with your supporting IDs and documents.
  2. Pay any applicable processing fee at the Treasurer’s window and keep your official receipt.
  1. Processing typically takes a few minutes to a couple of hours depending on office volume.
  2. Certain clearances require verification and signature by the Barangay Captain.
  1. Receive your signed and sealed document from the releasing desk.
  2. Verify that your name, address, date, and purpose are accurate before leaving.

Residents can request a Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, Certificate of Indigency, Barangay Business Clearance, Certificate of Good Moral Character, and other local certifications issued by the Barangay Hall.

Most standard barangay documents are processed within the same day, often within minutes to a couple of hours.

Fees depend on the type of document requested. First-time jobseekers (under RA 11261) and indigency certifications may be exempt from fees—check with the Barangay Hall staff for exact rates.

Yes, an immediate family member or authorized representative may apply on someone else’s behalf by presenting a signed authorization letter along with valid IDs of both the applicant and the representative.

For same-day requests, staff will call your name at the waiting area once signed. For requests requiring additional verification, the barangay office will advise you of the pickup schedule or contact you once ready.

Inform the Barangay Hall staff immediately so they can update your details before final printing and sealing.
