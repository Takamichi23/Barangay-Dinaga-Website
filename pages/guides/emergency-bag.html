How to prepare an

Emergency Go Bag

An emergency go bag is a portable kit stocked with survival essentials you can grab immediately during sudden evacuations—such as food, clean drinking water, medicines, and vital records. Regularly inspect expiration dates on food and medications and refresh items as needed.

Things to Pack in an Emergency Go Bag

  1. First Aid Kit
    • Bandages, rubbing alcohol, maintenance medications, paracetamol, antiseptic ointment, povidone-iodine, sterile gauze, and medical tape.
  2. Food & Water
    • Bottled drinking water, easy-open canned goods, biscuits/energy bars, and reusable utensils.
  3. Clothing & Warmth
    • Clean clothes for 2–3 days, slippers, blankets, raincoat or jacket, and spare undergarments.
  4. Lighting & Communication
    • Flashlight, fully charged powerbank, spare batteries, phone charger, whistle, and portable radio.
  5. Toiletries & Hygiene
    • Soap, shampoo, toothbrush, toothpaste, alcohol/sanitizer, face masks, and tissue paper.
  6. Identification & Cash
    • Valid IDs, photocopies of important family documents in a waterproof pouch, and emergency cash in small bills.
