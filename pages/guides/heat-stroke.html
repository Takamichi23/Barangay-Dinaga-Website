Tips to prevent

Heat Stroke

Preventing heat stroke—especially during intense summer heat or strenuous outdoor work—means taking proactive steps to keep your body cool and hydrated. Follow these key preventive measures:

  1. Stay Hydrated
    • Drink plenty of clean water throughout the day—even before you feel thirsty—especially during outdoor work or high heat indices.
    • Limit caffeine, sugary beverages, and alcohol, which contribute to dehydration.
    • Replenish electrolytes during prolonged physical exertion or heavy sweating.
  2. Wear Appropriate Clothing
    • Choose lightweight, breathable, loose-fitting fabrics that allow body heat to escape.
    • Opt for light-colored clothing that reflects sunlight instead of absorbing heat.
    • Use a wide-brimmed hat, umbrella, and UV-protective sunglasses when outdoors.
  3. Seek Shade & Cool Spaces
    • Avoid direct sun exposure during peak afternoon heat (typically 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
    • Ensure good indoor ventilation with fans or cooling areas.
    • Schedule strenuous farm work, errands, or exercise during cooler early morning or late afternoon hours.
  4. Take Regular Cooling Breaks
    • Rest frequently in shaded areas whenever working outdoors to let your body cool down.
    • Apply cool, damp towels to your neck, forehead, and wrists during breaks.
  5. Acclimatize Gradually
    • Ease into intense outdoor activity over several days so your body can safely adapt to warmer conditions.
