What to do in case of an

Earthquake

An earthquake occurs suddenly and without warning. Knowing how to prepare your home and respond calmly using “Duck, Cover, and Hold” can save lives.

Before

  1. Know the earthquake hazards in your area.
  2. Familiarize yourself with the following:
    • Fire extinguishers (Mga pang-apula ng apoy)
    • Medical kit (Mga gamit pang-medikal)
    • Exit routes (Ligtas na daanan palabas ng gusali)
    • Evacuation plan (Plano sa paglikas ng mga tao)
  3. Inspect your house and repair structural defects when necessary.
  4. Store harmful chemicals and flammable materials safely.
  5. Secure heavy furniture, shelves, and hanging objects.
  6. Prepare your family’s Emergency Go Bag containing survival essentials.
  7. Participate in school, office, and community earthquake drills.

During

When Inside a Building — “Duck, Cover, and Hold”

  1. Duck under a sturdy table and hold on tightly until the shaking stops.
  2. Stay away from glass windows, tall shelves, and heavy unsecured objects.
  3. Once the shaking stops, evacuate calmly and proceed to the designated open evacuation area.

When You Are Outside

  1. Move immediately to a wide open area.
  2. Stay away from buildings, trees, electric posts, and landslide-prone slopes.
  3. If inside a moving vehicle, pull over safely, stop, and exit away from overhead hazards.

After

  1. Stay alert for possible aftershocks.
  2. Check yourself and others for injuries and administer first aid if needed.
  3. Prioritize assisting senior citizens, pregnant women, persons with disabilities, and children.
  4. If in a coastal area with a tsunami threat, evacuate immediately to higher ground.
  5. Check for spills of toxic or flammable chemicals.
  6. Wait for authorities to declare structures safe before re-entering.
  7. Inspect for damaged water lines and LPG gas leaks.
