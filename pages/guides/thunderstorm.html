What to do in a

Thunderstorm

Thunderstorms are intense weather events bringing heavy downpours, gusty winds, thunder, and lightning—often causing power outages, localized flooding, and dangerous lightning strikes.

Before

  1. Monitor weather advisories and thunderstorm warnings from PAGASA and local authorities.
  2. Secure outdoor objects that could blow away or cause damage during strong gusts.
  3. Keep an emergency kit ready with flashlights, batteries, and first-aid supplies.
  4. Unplug sensitive appliances and electronics to protect them from power surges.
  5. Stay indoors and avoid open spaces whenever a thunderstorm warning is issued.

During

  1. Stay away from windows, doors, and conductive metal objects.
  2. Avoid using plugged-in electrical appliances or wired telephones.
  3. If caught outdoors, seek shelter inside a sturdy building or enclosed vehicle—avoid trees and utility poles.
  4. Never shelter beneath an isolated tree or stand in open rice fields.
  5. Avoid water-related activities, including swimming, wading, or bathing.

After

  1. Wait until the storm has fully passed before heading back outdoors.
  2. Watch out for downed power lines and report them immediately to CASURECO II or barangay officials.
  3. Inspect your surroundings for hazards such as broken glass, fallen branches, or standing water.
  4. Assist neighbors who need help while keeping your own safety first.
  5. Continue monitoring local updates for follow-up weather advisories.
