What to do in case of a

Fire Emergency

A fire emergency is any unplanned fire threatening life, property, and the surrounding community. Immediate, calm action is critical to prevent injury, loss of life, and widespread damage.

In Case of Fire

  1. Stay calm and evacuate the building immediately.
  2. Sound the fire alarm and contact the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) and barangay hotlines for assistance.
  3. If inside a multi-story building, always use the stairs—never use elevators.
  4. Before opening any closed door, feel the handle with the back of your hand. If it is hot, DO NOT OPEN as fire may be on the other side.
  5. If smoke is present, get down low and crawl while covering your mouth and nose with a damp cloth.
  6. If your clothes catch fire: STOP running, DROP to the floor, and ROLL to smother the flames.
  7. If trapped, stay inside the room, seal door gaps if possible, and signal for help at the window using bright cloth or loud noise.
  8. Once outside, never re-enter your house or building until fire authorities declare it safe.
  9. Have yourself and family members checked for burns or smoke inhalation.
