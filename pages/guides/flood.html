What to do in case of a

Flood

A flood occurs when water overflows onto normally dry land due to heavy rain, tidal surges, or overflowing waterways, posing serious risks to safety, health, and property.

Before

  1. Stay indoors and continuously monitor official weather and flood updates.
  2. Prepare an Emergency Go Bag stocked with food, clean water, medications, and a first-aid kit.
  3. Establish a family evacuation plan and identify higher, safe locations ahead of time.

During

  1. Move immediately to higher ground.
  2. Stay indoors and stay tuned to official barangay and weather advisories.
  3. Never touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in floodwater.
  4. Do not swim or go boating in swollen rivers or creeks.
  5. Do not cross flowing streams or roads where water rises above knee level.
  6. Avoid walking or driving through flooded areas.

After

  1. Do not switch on the main electrical breaker or use submerged appliances until inspected by an electrician.
  2. Thoroughly clean and disinfect surfaces and items contaminated by floodwater.
  3. If evacuated, wait for authorities to declare your home and area safe before returning.
