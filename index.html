About Dinaga

View About View Map Barangay Officials

Annual Census

Dinaga, formerly Poblacion, is a barangay in the municipality of Canaman, in the province of Camarines Sur. Its population as determined by the 2020 Census was 1,768, representing 4.88% of the total population of Canaman.
